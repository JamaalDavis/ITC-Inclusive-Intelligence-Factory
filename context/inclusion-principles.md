# Inclusive context principles

- Participation over assumptions: seek input from affected communities, paid when feasible.
- Accessibility is evaluated across tasks and user journeys, not just screenshots.
- Use WCAG 2.2 as a relevant baseline for applicable web content, not proof of inclusive service delivery.
- Treat disability and other sensitive information with data minimization and informed consent.
- Make machine uncertainty visible when it changes decisions.
- Make escalation, correction and appeal accessible, timely and meaningful.
- Consider whose incentives are optimized and who bears the cost of failure.
- Respect multilingual, assistive technology and low-bandwidth pathways.
