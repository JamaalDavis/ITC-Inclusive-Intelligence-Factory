# ITC quality rubric (pilot)

1. **Evidence**: Every factual claim has a supporting inspected source; compare claim/source match.
2. **Accessibility**: Review semantic structure, reading order, image alternatives, cognitive clarity and reachable human alternatives.
3. **Human agency**: Consequential action requires user authority and recovery/appeal path.
4. **Representation**: Avoid assumptions about demographics or disability; include affected perspectives where relevant.
5. **Editorial quality**: Voice, clarity, structure, nuance and usefulness.
6. **Operations**: Cost, reviewer corrections, elapsed time, failed/blocked actions.

Scores: 0 = fail, 1 = partial, 2 = pass. A numeric total is never a substitute for blocking critical defects or explicit human approval. All machine-detected scores require manual validation on a pilot sample.
